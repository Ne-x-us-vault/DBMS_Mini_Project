# Split Chat

A super simple app to **track shared expenses** (with optional splitting) and **chat**. No accounts, no backend — everything stays on your phone.

## What it does

- **Splits tab (main)** — one shared expense list. **Anyone can add, edit, or delete any expense.**
- **Splitting is optional** — an expense can just be tracked (who paid, how much, when) without being split between people.
- **History** — every add / edit / delete is logged **with the person's name**, so you can see who did what.
- **Who owes whom** — only for split expenses, the app works out balances and lists the fewest transfers to settle up.
- **Chat tab** — simple group chat.
- Everything is saved on your phone — close the app and your data stays.

## How it works (in plain words)

1. **Managing as** — pick your name (top-right person icon) so history shows *who* did what.
2. **People** — add everyone involved. Just a name.
3. **Expenses** — tap + on the shared list. Type what, how much, and the date.
   - Toggle **"Split this expense"** on to share it among people (equal or manual shares).
   - Leave it off for **tracking only** — a plain record with no splitting.
4. **History** — tap any expense to see its full history, like *"Meera added Lunch"* then *"Aarav changed the amount"*. The main **History** section also logs every add, edit, and delete across all expenses.
5. **The math** — split bills are divided among their people and compared with who paid. Money is counted in paise (integers), so calculations stay exact.
6. **The result** — balances show who's owed money (green) and who owes (red), then "who owes whom" as the fewest transfers possible.
